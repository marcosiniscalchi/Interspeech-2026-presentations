# Interspeech 2026 — Presentation Slides

**Sabato Marco Siniscalchi**
Professor of Computer Engineering, University of Palermo
Speech Technology Laboratory

This repository contains the presentation slides for my talks at **Interspeech 2026**, held in Sydney, Australia.

## Presentations

### 1. MDM-ASR: Bridging Accuracy and Efficiency in ASR with Diffusion-Based Non-Autoregressive Decoding

This presentation explores diffusion-based non-autoregressive automatic speech recognition (ASR), focusing on the relationship between recognition accuracy and computational efficiency, and on bridging the gap between non-autoregressive and autoregressive approaches.

* [View slides (PDF)](MDM-ASR.pdf)

### 2. Monaural Speech Enhancement: From Shallow Networks to Speech Foundation Models

This presentation reviews the evolution of monaural speech enhancement, from traditional shallow neural networks to modern deep learning approaches and speech foundation models.

* [View slides (PDF)](SurveyTalk.pdf)
